# Data Licenses, Code Licensing & Attribution Governance — Nepal TRĀYA 2.0

**Nepal TRĀYA (त्राय)** strictly separates the license of its **original repository source code** from the diverse licenses, attribution obligations, and statutory governance rules of **upstream scientific, Earth-observation, and Government of Nepal datasets**.

> **Important Governance Notice:**
> The repository's root [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) (MIT License) applies **only** to original source code, documentation, and templates authored for Nepal TRĀYA. It does **not** re-license third-party vendored libraries, open geospatial databases, satellite imagery archives, or Government of Nepal statutory data feeds. Anyone forking, redistributing, or building commercial services on derived data in `data/` or `cache/` must comply with every applicable upstream license listed below.

## 3. Government of Nepal Statutory Data Governance & Non-Redistribution Rules

Because **NDRRMA (BIPAD Portal)**, **DHM**, **DMG/NEMRC**, and **DoR** are sovereign statutory agencies of the Government of Nepal — not open-source CC0 mirrors — TRĀYA 2.0 enforces five non-negotiable governance guardrails:

1. **No Implicit Open License Claim:** Publicly accessible endpoints (such as `https://bipadportal.gov.np/api/v1/`) are consumed strictly as **read-only public statutory context**. TRĀYA does not claim MIT, CC BY, or ODbL ownership over Government of Nepal records, and forbids bulk repackaging or commercial resale of raw BIPAD dumps via this repository.
2. **Strict Official-vs-Modelled UI Separation:** Official statutory alerts, verified BIPAD incidents, and DHM gauge statuses are stored in dedicated namespaces and rendered in a visually distinct **"Official Statutory Agency"** lane — never merged into or disguised as TRĀYA's modelled GloFAS, Dahal–Hasegawa, GLOF, or cascade watch tiers.
3. **Verbatim Integrity & Attribution:** Agency names, official Nepali (`titleNe`) and English (`title`) strings, verification flags (`verified`, `approved`), timestamps in `NPT (UTC+05:45)`, and direct deep links back to `bipadportal.gov.np`, `hydrology.gov.np`, `seismonepal.gov.np`, and `dor.gov.np` must be preserved without editorial alteration of warning severity.
4. **No Insecure Hotlinking or Unauthorized Load:** Legacy HTTP media URLs embedded in BIPAD telemetry payloads (e.g., `http://daq.hydrology.gov.np/images/...`) are **stripped and never loaded** in TRĀYA's strict-CSP HTTPS frontend. Scheduled polling is throttled, cached, and identified via an explicit `User-Agent` header so TRĀYA never imposes peak-monsoon load on statutory agency infrastructure.
5. **Non-Endorsement & MOU Boundary:** Displaying public statutory links or read-only BIPAD metadata does **not** imply formal Government of Nepal endorsement, operational SLA, or statutory alerting authority. Any future operational alerting or authenticated agency feed integration (Wave D / `v3.0-pilot`) requires an executed Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) as specified in [`docs/INSTITUTIONAL_INTEGRATION_SPEC.md`](docs/INSTITUTIONAL_INTEGRATION_SPEC.md).

## 4. License Compatibility Matrix for Forks & Downstream Users

| Intended Downstream Use | Permitted Out-of-the-Box? | Required Actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Academic research, teaching, thesis, peer-reviewed publication** | **Yes** | Retain MIT notice for code; cite all relevant upstream datasets and peer-reviewed papers (Section 5); preserve non-operational safety disclaimer. |
| **Open-source / NGO non-commercial fork on GitHub Pages** | **Yes** | Keep `data/faults.json` under **CC BY-SA 4.0**; keep OSM derivatives under **ODbL 1.0**; keep HydroRIVERS derivatives non-commercial; respect GoN non-redistribution rules and strict official-vs-modelled separation. |
| **Commercial SaaS, proprietary GIS product, or paid risk analytics** | **Partial — requires replacements/licenses** | MIT code may be reused, **but** you must: (1) obtain commercial licenses/keys for **HydroRIVERS/HydroSHEDS**, **Open-Meteo**, and **Esri**; (2) verify **ICIMOD RDS** commercial terms; (3) satisfy **ODbL 1.0** (OSM) and **CC BY-SA 4.0** (GEM Faults) share-alike clauses or remove those layers; and (4) remove or obtain written GoN authorization for **BIPAD/DHM/DMG** data. |
| **Operational public early warning or life-safety alerting** | **No (Prohibited without statutory MOU)** | TRĀYA is a screening observatory, not an EWS. Operational alerting in Nepal is the legal mandate of **DHM**, **DMG/NEMRC**, and **NDRRMA** under the Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act, 2074 (2017). |
