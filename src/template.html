Plain-Language Preparedness Reminders

  • GLOF / River Flash Flood: If water turns suddenly muddy/debris-laden or river level drops abruptly and roars, move immediately to high ground perpendicular to the river valley — do not wait to cross bridges.
  • Earthquake: Drop, Cover, and Hold On away from masonry walls and windows. After strong shaking (M≥5), stay alert for aftershocks, landslides on steep slopes, and sudden glacial/river surges.
  • Monsoon Landslide: Watch for new ground cracks, bulging slopes, tilting trees, or muddy spring water after heavy 24–72 h rain; relocate early to safer open ground.
