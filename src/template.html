What this is

A screening assessment of 1,429 Nepalese glacial lakes built entirely from published methods and open data, combined with live earthquake and precipitation feeds. Every number is reproducible from the pipeline in src/.

It is not a forecast and not an operational warning service. See the Methods and limits tab.
